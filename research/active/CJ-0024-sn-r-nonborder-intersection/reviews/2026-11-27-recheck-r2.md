  - T_c is a t-norm. M(x,y) = max(0, x+y−c) is associative, both sides being
    max(0, x+y+z−2c). It is not left-continuous at (x,1) for 0 < x < 1,

    - If c−x+y < 1 the sup is attained.

## Task 3 — the Corollary

- **Problem 4.2(i): "yes".** I_c and J_c are (S,N)-implications. Each is
  I_T for a t-norm T that is discontinuous at boundary points (x,1), which is
  non-border-continuity under both readings of the definition. Each violates
  (OP), so by Lemma 4 neither is the residual of any left-continuous t-norm.
  Each differs from I_WB. This follows.
- **Problem 4.8.1 equality false.** Under either reading of I_T*, I_c ∈
  I_{S,N} ∩ (I_T \ I_{T_LC}) ⊆ I_{S,N} ∩ (I_T \ I_{T*}), so the equality fails.
  This follows.
- **Problem 4.5(ii), first question, strong reading: "no".** I_c and J_c are
  QL-implications that are residuals of non-left-continuous t-norms and of no
  left-continuous one. This follows.
- The note makes no claim for the literal reading, and no claim of
  characterization, novelty or cardinality beyond ≥ 𝔠.
