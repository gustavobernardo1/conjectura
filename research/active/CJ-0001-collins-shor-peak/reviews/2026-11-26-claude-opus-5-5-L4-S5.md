## Statement as read from the primary source

Primary source: Conjecture 1.2 of arXiv:1507.02341. The reviewer did **not**
open the arXiv paper. The statement was taken from the verbatim quotation in
`STATE.md`: "The [normalized] coefficients of the distance characteristic
polynomial for any tree `T` with `n` vertices are unimodal with peak between
`⌊n/2⌋` and `⌈n − n/√5⌉`." Here `d_k = 2^{k−(n−2)} |δ_k|`, `0 ≤ k ≤ n − 2`,
and `δ_k` is the coefficient of `x^k` in `det(D − xI)`. The lower half (every
maximizing index is `≥ m = ⌊n/2⌋`) is equivalent to `d_{m−1} < d_m` (R1, first
bullet, using the log-concavity theorem of the source). Neither claim reviewed
here resolves the conjecture. Each is a supporting statement:

**L4 as stated (written down before the proof was examined).** Hypotheses:
`s ≥ 1` (implicitly); `f : ℝ^s → ℝ` a polynomial of degree at most 1 in each
variable; `λ ∈ ℤ^s`; `S ∈ ℤ` with `S > Σ λ_i`; for every pair `i ≠ k` and every
**real** `x ≥ λ` (coordinatewise), `∂²f/∂x_i∂x_k (x) ≥ 0`. Conclusion: for
every **integer** `c ∈ ℤ^s` with `c ≥ λ` and `Σ c_i = S`,
`f(c) ≥ min_{1≤p≤s} f(λ + (S − Σλ_i) e_p)`.

**S5 as stated.** Hypothesis: the skeleton tree `S` (on `s ≥ 2` vertices) has
Property Γ, meaning that `r_A` has a non-negative half-gamma expansion of
degree `s − |A| + 1` for every `A ⊆ V` with `|A| ≥ 2`. Conclusions, for every
`n ≥ 2s`: (i) `β_A(n) ≥ 0` for `|A| ≥ 2`; (ii) `∂²D_n/∂c_u∂c_v ≥ 0` for all
`u ≠ v` at every real `c ≥ 0`; (iii) for every `c ∈ ℤ_{≥0}^V` with
`Σ c_v = n − s`, `d_m(S(c)) − d_{m−1}(S(c)) ≥ min_{p∈V} D_n((n − s) e_p)`. By
S1, `D_n((n−s)e_p)` is `d_m − d_{m−1}` of the tree `S((n−s)e_p)`.

## Verdict

- **Lemma L4: `no gap found within stated scope`.** Scope: the lemma exactly
  as stated (multi-affine `f`, integer `λ`, integer `S > Σλ`, mixed partials
  non-negative at real `x ≥ λ`, conclusion at integer points of the slice),
  including the boundary cases listed. The verdict also covers the other
  derivations in `vertex-principle.md` that are presented as proved: the
  conditional Proposition (given V(s) and L3′/S1) and the two-line
  top-coefficient computation. It does not cover Hypothesis V(s), L3′ (a
  sketch, superseded by S1), the positivity of the vertex families, or the
  claimed second derivation of T2 and T3. The V(3) table was reproduced
  independently, but its use for T2 and T3 also rests on unreviewed vertex
  polynomials. Defect D1 (stale statements about L3′ and V(s) relative to
  `skeleton-identity.md`) is documentary and does not affect L4.
- **Corollary S5: `no gap found within stated scope`.** Scope: the implication
  "Property Γ for `S` ⇒ (i), (ii), (iii) for every `n ≥ 2s`", using S1 to S4 as
  stated at this revision and L4 as above. S5 is conditional, and Property Γ
  is not proved in general. For the 47 skeletons with `s ≤ 8` it was confirmed
  by the reviewer's independent exact computation, which is finite. The
  conclusion is a non-strict comparison with the "skeleton plus one star"
  trees and proves no positivity by itself. Defect D2 (the remark under
  Conjecture Γ also covers trees with one non-leaf vertex, `P_3` in
  particular, which lie outside the setting) is a wording defect outside the
  corollary.
