### Defects of wording (not mathematical gaps)

1. **S21, proof**: "`H = …`, of degree `q` with leading coefficient
   `Π(n_0 + σ)`" and "`G = …`, of degree `q + 1` with leading coefficient
   `Π(n_0 + 2σ)`" are false when `n_0 = σ = 0` (all weights zero:
   `H = q(1 + x)^{q−1}`, `G = 0`). The proof only needs "coefficient of `x^q`"
   and "coefficient of `x^{q+1}`", which are correct in all cases.
2. **Γ_w paragraph**: "strictly larger than `C_d`" is justified only for
   `d ≥ 2`; for `d = 1` the palindromic argument gives nothing (strictness
   holds by `f = −1`). The paragraph might also say that this cone contains
   polynomials with negative coefficients (for `d = 1`), which makes the
   contrast with `C_d` sharper.

   requires `ν ≠ 0`, while the parts of a merge may carry the zero weight
   (the single vertex of weight 0, a tree planted on a zero-weight branch),
   for which `R_k` is not in any cone (one vertex of weight 0:
   `R = −(1 + 2x)²`). The remark is not used in any proof.
4. **"What this refutes"**: "in every counterexample above, `E`, `R` and `Λ`
