## Statement as read from the primary sources

- **Definition (S4 section of `skeleton-identity.md`).** `r` has a *non-negative half-gamma expansion of degree `d`* if `r = Σ_{2e<d} ρ_e x^e(1+x)^{d−2e} + Σ_{d≤2e≤2d} σ_e x^e` with all `ρ_e, σ_e ≥ 0`. The expansion is unique.
- **Property Γ (same file).** For every `A ⊆ V` with `|A| ≥ 2`, `r_A` has such an expansion of degree `s − |A| + 1`. **Conjecture Γ (MD-0010):** every tree with `s ≥ 2` has Property Γ.
- **Proposition S3.** For every tree with `s ≥ 2`, `r_V(x) = (s+3)x + s`.
- **Proposition S10 (`skeleton-locality.md`).** This covers trees with `s ≥ 2` and `A` such that `B = V ∖ A` is non-empty and independent. Nothing in it requires `|A| ≥ 2`.
- **Γ⁺ (revised note).** The same as Property Γ, but required for every `A` with `|A| ≥ 1`.
