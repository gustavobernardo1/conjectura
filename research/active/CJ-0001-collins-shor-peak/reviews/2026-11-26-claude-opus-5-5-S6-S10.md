## Statement as read from the primary source

Written from `skeleton-identity.md` before reading the proofs.

- `S` is a tree on `s ≥ 2` vertices, with distance matrix `P`. For `A ⊆ V` we have `a = |A|` and `B = V ∖ A`, `t = s − a`. For `x ≠ 0`,
  `N_A(x) = 2x I_B − P − 1_A 1ᵀ − 1 1_Aᵀ − (a/2x) J` and
  `r_A(x) = −2^{2−s} x det N_A(x)`.
- *Non-negative half-gamma expansion of degree `d`* (of a polynomial `r` of degree `≤ d`):
  `r = Σ_{e ≥ 0, 2e < d} ρ_e x^e (1+x)^{d−2e} + Σ_{d ≤ 2e ≤ 2d} σ_e x^e`, with every `ρ_e, σ_e ≥ 0`. The basis elements are `x^e(1+x)^{d−2e}` for `0 ≤ e < d/2` and `x^e` for `⌈d/2⌉ ≤ e ≤ d`. The expansion is unique.
- *Property Γ* is a property of the skeleton `S`. It holds if, for every `A ⊆ V` with `|A| ≥ 2`, `r_A` has such an expansion of degree `d = s − |A| + 1 = t + 1`.
- Under review: the claim that `r_A` is a polynomial of degree `≤ t+1`; S6 (both the formula and locality); S7 (both forms, and `r_A(0) = a`); S8; S9; Lemma C; S10 (both the identity and `a − H ≥ 1`); and the sentence that Property Γ holds whenever the complement of `A` is independent.
