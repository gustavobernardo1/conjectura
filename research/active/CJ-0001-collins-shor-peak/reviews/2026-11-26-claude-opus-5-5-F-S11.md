## Statement as read from the primary source

From `skeleton-identity.md`, section "Proposition S4": a polynomial `r` of degree at most `d` has a *non-negative half-gamma expansion of degree `d`* if
`r = Σ_{2e<d} ρ_e x^e(1+x)^{d−2e} + Σ_{d≤2e≤2d} σ_e x^e` with all `ρ_e, σ_e ≥ 0`. This basis is a basis of the polynomials of degree `≤ d`, so the expansion is unique.
*Property Γ* for a tree `S` means: for every `A ⊆ V` with `|A| ≥ 2`, `r_A` has such an expansion of degree `s − |A| + 1`.
*Conjecture Γ*: every tree with `s ≥ 2` has Property Γ.
From `skeleton-locality.md` I used only these statements.
**S7, second form:** for every tree on `s ≥ 2` vertices and every `A ⊆ V`, `r_A = Φ_B[a(1+x) + (3+3H−2e_B)x] − x² zᵀadj(I+xL_B)z`. The right-hand side depends on `(S, A)` only through `a` and the data `L_B`, `H`, `e_B`, `z` of `B`.
**Lemma C:** `x^e(1+x)^j` with integers `e, j ≥ 0`, `e+j ≤ d`, `2e+j ≥ d` has a non-negative half-gamma expansion of degree `d`, and so do non-negative combinations of such polynomials.
The notation: `L_B = L[B,B]`, so its diagonal holds the degrees `g_w` in `S` and its off-diagonal entries are −1 on the edges inside `B`; `h_w = g_w − 1`, `H = Σ_B h_w`, `k_w` = neighbours in `B`, `e_B` = edges inside `B`, `z_w = 2h_w − k_w`, `Φ_B = det(I + xL_B)`.

The claims reviewed, read before their proofs:
- **Cones:** `C_d` is the cone generated by `x^e(1+x)^i` with `e+i ≤ d` and `2e+i ≥ d`. Claimed: `C_{d1}C_{d2} ⊆ C_{d1+d2}`; `xC_d, x²C_d ⊆ C_{d+2}`; `1 ∈ C_0`; `(1+x)+νx ∈ C_1` for real `ν ≥ 0`; `α(1+x)+βx ∈ C_1` for `α, β ≥ 0`.
- **Lemma F:** for every tree `T` on `j ≥ 1` vertices and every weight `ν: W → Z_{≥0}` that is not identically zero, `det(I + x(L_T + diag ν)) ∈ C_j`.
- **Corollary F′:** for a tree with `s ≥ 2` and `1 ≤ a ≤ s−1`: `Φ_B ∈ C_t`; `3+3H−2e_B` is an integer `≥ 3`; `Φ_B[a(1+x)+(3+3H−2e_B)x] ∈ C_{t+1}`.
- **S11:** same hypotheses. Contracting the components of `S[A]` gives a tree `S′` on `a′+t ≥ 2` vertices, in which `A′` is independent and its complement is `B`. Moreover `r_A^S = r_{A′}^{S′} + (a−a′)(1+x)Φ_B` with `(1+x)Φ_B ∈ C_{t+1}`. Hence an expansion for `(S′, A′)` gives one for `(S, A)`, in the same degree `t+1`.
- **What this gives:** Γ⁺ means Γ with `|A| ≥ 1`. The paragraph claims: (i) Γ⁺ for all trees ⇒ Conjecture Γ; (ii) by S11, Γ⁺ for all trees ⇐ Γ⁺ for all pairs with `A` independent; (iii) for independent `A`, `a = Σ_B ν_w − c + 1`; (iv) `|A| = 1` is needed because a connected `A` contracts to one vertex; (v) by S10 the pairs with `B` independent are done, and what remains is `A` independent with `e_B ≥ 1`.
