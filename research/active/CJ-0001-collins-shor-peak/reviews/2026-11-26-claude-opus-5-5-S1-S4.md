## Statement as read from the primary source

From arXiv:1507.02341v3, Section 1 and Observation 3.1. I wrote this down before reading the proofs.

- `D(T)` is the distance matrix of a tree `T` on `n` vertices. `δ_k(T)` is the coefficient of `x^k` in `det(D − xI) = (−1)^n det(xI − D)`.
- For `0 ≤ k ≤ n−2`: `d_k(T) = 2^k |δ_k(T)| / 2^{n−2}`. By Fact 1.1 (Graham–Lovász eq. (44)), `(−1)^{n−1} δ_k(T) > 0` for `0 ≤ k ≤ n−2`, so `d_k = (−1)^{n−1} δ_k / 2^{n−2−k}`.
- Observation 3.1: `ℓ_T(x) = −2^{−(n−2)} det(2xI − D(T))` is a real-rooted polynomial. Its coefficient of `x^n` is `−4`, its coefficient of `x^{n−1}` is `0`, and its coefficient of `x^k` is `d_k(T) > 0` for `0 ≤ k ≤ n−2`.
- My own derivation: `det(2xI − D) = (−1)^n Σ_k δ_k 2^k x^k`, so `−2^{−(n−2)} det(2xI − D) = Σ_k (−1)^{n−1} δ_k 2^{k−(n−2)} x^k`, which agrees with the above. The note's `ℓ_T` and `d_k` (lines 11, 61–64, and `lemmas.md` lines 14–18) match the source exactly, including signs and powers of 2.
- Conjecture 1.2 (Collins–Shor), for context only: the peak of `d_0, …, d_{n−2}` lies between `⌊n/2⌋` and `⌈n − n/√5⌉`. S1–S4 are auxiliary statements, not this conjecture. The note does not claim the conjecture.

The statements under review, as written in the note:

- **Setting.** `S` is a tree on `s ≥ 2` vertices with vertex set `V` and distance matrix `P`. `c ∈ Z_{≥0}^V`. `S(c)` is `S` with `c_v` pendant leaves attached at each `v`, so `n = s + Σ c_v`. `Q(c)` is the `2s × 2s` matrix of lines 28–31, `G(c; x) = det(2xI − Q(c))`, and `−G/4^{s−1} = Σ_{j=0}^{2s} g_j(c) x^j`.
- **S1.** If `n ≥ 2s`, then `ℓ_{S(c)}(x) = (x+1)^{n−2s} Σ_j g_j(c) x^j` (zeros among the `c_v` allowed). Consequently `d_k(S(c)) = Σ_j g_j(c) C(n−2s, k−j)` for `0 ≤ k ≤ n−2`.
- **S2.** `G(c; x) = Σ_{A⊆V} c^A γ_A(x)` with `γ_A(x) = (2x+2)^{s−a} (2x)^a det N_A(x)` for `x ≠ 0`, where `N_A = 2x I_Ā − P − 1_A 1ᵀ − 1 1_Aᵀ − (a/2x) J`.
- **S3.** For every tree `S` on `s ≥ 2` vertices, `r_V(x) = (s+3)x + s`. Equivalently, `c^V` appears only in `g_{s−1}` (coefficient `s`) and in `g_s` (coefficient `s+3`).
- **S4.** If `r_A` has a non-negative half-gamma expansion of degree `d = s − a + 1`, then `β_A(n) ≥ 0` for every `n ≥ 2s`, where `m = ⌊n/2⌋` and `β_A(n) = ([x^m] − [x^{m−1}]) x^{a−1} (1+x)^{n−s−a} r_A(x)`.
